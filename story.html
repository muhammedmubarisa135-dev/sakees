TRADITION & HERITAGE

More Than A Pickle.

"For us, pickle is more than a side dish. It is the burst of flavour that brings life to a simple meal."

THE IDEA

SAKKEES brings the authentic taste of Kerala's traditional kitchen into a modern jar. Every batch is prepared with carefully selected ingredients, authentic spices, and recipes inspired by generations of home cooking.

THE FLAVOUR

From rich slow-cooked beef pickles and aromatic roasted chicken to tangy sun-cured lemons and juicy fresh prawns, our pickles are designed to bring nostalgia to your dining table.

THE PROCESS

Handmade in small controlled batches with zero artificial preservatives or colors. Every jar undergoes strict hygienic preparation and careful packaging to seal in authentic flavour.

THE HOME & THE JAR

Whether you are sitting at home in Kerala or miles away, opening a jar of SAKKEES is designed to take you back home.
